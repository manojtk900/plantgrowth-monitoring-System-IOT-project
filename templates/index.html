Zero ESP32 Firmware Modification:

The ESP32 continues sending raw 12-bit ADC values (moisture_raw). Flask safely converts raw → calibrated RSMI % on the backend. Moisture calculation remains independent from temperature. Temperature dynamically modulates drying risk and condition score without altering raw moisture values.

Sensor ESP32 Pin Signal Type

DS18B20 Temp Probe GPIO 4 1-Wire (4.7kΩ Pull-up)

HC-SR04 Sonic Echo GPIO 18 2.5V Voltage Divider
